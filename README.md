## 🔬 Mission Overview

**Project OpenArchosaur** is an international, decentralized, open-source citizen-science initiative dedicated to executing **Phylogenetic Genome Rehearsal and Reversals**. Our long-term objective is to mathematically reconstruct, validate, and compile a 99.9% accurate (Q30 Standard) digital genomic blueprint of a large Cretaceous theropod dinosaur (*Tyrannosaurus rex* lineage).

Because deep time has degraded natural fossilized DNA past the point of organic recovery, traditional extraction is impossible. However, because modern avian species *are* living coelurosaurian dinosaurs, their streamlined genomes contain the silent, deactivated, and compacted genetic architecture of their ancestors. 

By treating evolution as an invertible mathematical matrix and applying **Ancestral Sequence Reconstruction (ASR)** via open-source machine learning, this project aims to bridge the 66-million-year gap using public data, crowdsourced computing, and distributed peer review.

## 🛠️ The 4-Input Architecture Matrix

To reach a mathematically verified sequence without direct genetic source material, our core pipeline utilizes a four-pronged data ingestion engine:

### 1. The High-Fidelity Phylogenetic Bracket
We utilize long-read, error-corrected genomic data from a highly targeted master panel of living Archosaur descendants to isolate the baseline ancestral genome framework.
*   **Basal Framework:** *Struthio camelus* (Ostrich) & *Dromaius novaehollandiae* (Emu) — chosen for low baseline rates of molecular drift and preserved non-avian karyotype architectures.
*   **Atavistic Switchboard:** *Gallus gallus* (Chicken) — chosen for highly mapped, structurally stable regulatory pathways controlling embryonic bone and tooth morphogenesis.
*   **Anatomical Links:** *Opisthocomus hoazin* (Hoatzin) — utilized to map active, non-fused embryonic wing-claw pathways.
*   **The Outgroup Control:** *Alligator mississippiensis* (American Alligator) — utilized to inject the heavy-boned, land-dwelling, uncompacted structural sequences that flying birds shed during evolution.

### 2. Biophysical Mutation Modeling
Every reconstructed ancestral sequence must strictly obey the laws of thermodynamics, molecular stability, and structural biology. Reconstructed sequences are continuously validated through open-source variational autoencoders and evolutionary scale models (e.g., ESM and open-source ASR frameworks) to filter out non-viable protein "hallucinations."

### 3. Deep-Time Fossil Proteomics (The Validation Checklist)
While DNA degrades completely, durable structural proteins can endure. We ingest mass spectrometry records of verified Mesozoic biomolecules—such as the 68-million-year-old *T. rex* collagen peptide fragments—to act as physical calibration nodes [science.sciencemag.org]. If a mathematically projected gene sequence generates proteins that do not match real-world fossil fragments, the AI's mutation trajectory calculation is automatically adjusted and rerun.

### 4. Morphological Shape-to-Gene Inverse Design
We train localized Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) on public veterinary datasets linking bone morphology directly to gene expression [science.sciencemag.org]. The AI treats high-resolution 3D fossil scans (e.g., *T. rex* specimens "Sue" and "Stan") as a structural destination, calculating the precise regulatory switch adjustments and sequence variations required to morph standard avian skeletal geometry into heavy theropod structures.

## 🧬 Current Phase: Core Data Assembly

We are currently operating in **Phase 1: Core Data Assembly**. We do not require wet-lab work or private capital. We are aggregating the foundational global datasets and building the automated testing workflows.

### How to Contribute Right Now:

#### 💻 For Machine Learning & Bioinformatics Engineers:
*   Help optimize the API data-ingestion scripts inside the `/data/genomes/` folder to pull error-corrected long-read sequencing data seamlessly from NCBI GenBank.
*   Design the automated **GitHub Actions CI/CD workflows** that will pass incoming code pull-requests through open-source protein validation models to check for molecular stability.

#### 🦖 For Paleontologists & Data Curators:
*   Contribute to the `/data/morphology/` index by sourcing public-domain, high-resolution 3D CT scans of theropod fossils from repositories like MorphoSource.
*   Help catalog clean, peer-reviewed mass spectrometry data of Cretaceous protein remnants into `/data/proteomics/`.

#### 🐓 For Citizen Scientists & Poultry Breeders:
*   Review the `/registry/atavism_tracker/` workspace. We are compiling guidelines for backyard poultry hobbyists to report, document, and cross-reference naturally occurring ancestral mutations (such as spontaneous wing claws or unusual scale configurations) to help map out natural selection throwbacks.

## 🛡️ Security, Integrity, and Code Governance

1.  **Strict GPG Signing:** To protect the project from hijacking, malicious script injections, or false updates, **all commits to the main branch must be cryptographically signed with a GPG key**. Unverified commits will be rejected automatically by our branch protection rules.
2.  **Modular Future-Proof Architecture:** All software scripts must be completely modular. Do not tie workflows to proprietary platforms. The infrastructure must be built to scale seamlessly as public, open-source AI models grow exponentially more powerful over time.
3.  **The Anti-Monopoly Mandate:** Under the Apache 2.0 license, this blueprint belongs to humanity [opensource.org]. Any derivative pipelines, software forks, or structural data generated using this repository *must* remain entirely open-source, public domain, and completely free of restrictive private patents.
